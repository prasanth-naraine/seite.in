Frequently Asked Questions
1. Do small businesses actually need a website?
Yes. Most customers check online before visiting or buying. Without a website, you lose them to competitors who show up first. Your website is a 24/7 working shop.
2. How much do digital marketing solutions cost?
Cost depends on what your business needs. Seite offers plans for small budgets and larger ones, so you only pay for what fits. Check our pricing.
3. What is SEO?
SEO means optimizing your website so it shows up higher on Google when people search for things related to your business.
4. Is it really possible to rank on the first page of Google?
Yes, with steady SEO work over a few months based on the business type. It's not instant, but it builds real, lasting visibility.
5. How much does a website really cost?
It depends on how many pages and features you need. Pricing starts at ₹2,500 per page and gets cheaper as your site grows. Check our pricing.
6. What's the biggest challenge businesses face with marketing?
Trying to do everything at once without a clear plan. We help you focus on what actually works for your budget first.
7. Do I need a website if I already have social media?
Yes. Social media pages can get restricted or lost, but your website is fully yours and always under your control.
8. How much does social media marketing cost?
Plans start at ₹1,000 per month for content, with ad management available separately. Check our pricing.
9. How often should a business post on social media?
Consistency matters more than frequency. A steady posting schedule works better than posting a lot, then going quiet.
10. How much does logo design cost?
Plans start at ₹4,000 for small businesses, going up to ₹25,000+ for a full brand identity package. Check our pricing.
11. Can I get more than one service together?
Yes. Many businesses combine a website with SEO and social media to grow faster, and we can build a plan around that.
12. Is PPC better than SEO?
They work differently. PPC gets you seen right away but costs per click. SEO takes longer but keeps working without ongoing ad spend.
