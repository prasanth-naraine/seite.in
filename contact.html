Frequently Asked Questions
1. Do small businesses actually need a website?
Yes. Most customers check online before visiting or buying. Without a website, you may lose potential customers to competitors who show up first. Your website works as a 24/7 online presence for your business.
2. How much do digital marketing solutions cost?
Cost depends on what your business needs. Seite offers plans for small budgets as well as larger marketing requirements, so you only pay for what fits your business. Check our pricing for more details.
3. What is SEO?
SEO means Search Engine Optimization. It involves optimizing your website so it can appear higher on Google and other search engines when people search for products or services related to your business.
4. Is it really possible to rank on the first page of Google?
Yes, but SEO takes time and consistent effort. Depending on your business type, competition, website, and target keywords, meaningful improvements can take several months. SEO is not instant, but consistent optimization can build long-term search visibility.
5. How much does a website really cost?
Website costs depend on the number of pages, design requirements, and features you need. Pricing starts at ₹2,500 per page and becomes more cost-effective as your website grows. Check our pricing for more details.
6. What's the biggest challenge businesses face with marketing?
One of the biggest challenges is trying to do everything at once without a clear plan. We help you focus on the marketing activities that make sense for your business and budget first.
7. Do I need a website if I already have social media?
Yes. Social media is useful for reaching and engaging customers, but your website gives you a digital presence that you fully control. Social media accounts can be restricted, changed, or lost, while your website remains your own online platform.
8. How much does social media marketing cost?
Social media marketing plans start at ₹1,000 per month for content. Ad management is available separately depending on your requirements. Check our pricing for more details.
9. How often should a business post on social media?
Consistency matters more than posting frequency. A steady posting schedule that you can maintain is generally more effective than posting heavily for a short period and then going quiet.
10. How much does logo design cost?
Logo design plans start at ₹4,000 for small businesses and can go up to ₹25,000 or more for a complete brand identity package. Check our pricing for more details.
11. Can I get more than one service together?
Yes. Many businesses combine services such as website development, SEO, and social media marketing. We can create a plan around your business goals and marketing requirements.
12. Is PPC better than SEO?
PPC and SEO work differently. PPC can help you get visibility quickly, but you pay for clicks or other advertising activity. SEO generally takes longer to build but can provide ongoing organic visibility without paying for every click. The right approach depends on your goals, timeline, and budget.
