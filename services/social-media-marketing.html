Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is social media marketing?
It's how businesses use platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn to reach customers, build trust, and drive sales.
2. How much does social media marketing cost?
Plans start at ₹1,000/month for content, with ad management available separately if you want to run paid campaigns.
3. How often should a business post on social media?
Consistency matters more than frequency. A steady posting schedule works better than posting a lot, then going quiet.
4. Which social media platform is best for my business?
It depends on your audience. Instagram and Facebook work well for visual, consumer-facing businesses, while LinkedIn suits B2B and professional services. We help you figure out where your customers actually spend time.
5. How do I choose a social media marketing agency?
Look for a team that's transparent about pricing, shows real content examples, and explains their strategy clearly, not just posting for the sake of posting.
6. How can social media generate leads?
Through consistent content, engaging with your audience, and running targeted social media advertising campaigns that reach people actually interested in what you offer.
