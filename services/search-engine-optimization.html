Frequently Asked Questions
1. Do I need a website before starting SEO?
Yes. SEO improves your website's visibility on Google, so a website is required first. If you don't have one, we can build it as part of your plan.
2. What is SEO?
SEO means optimizing your website so it shows up higher on Google when people search for things related to your business.
3. How long does SEO take to show results?
Usually a few months. SEO is a steady, long-term process, not an overnight fix.
4. How much do SEO services cost?
Plans start at ₹10,000 per month, depending on how much support and reporting you need.
5. What is local SEO?
It helps your business show up when people nearby search for what you offer, especially useful for local shops and services, including Google Maps SEO and local business SEO visibility.
6. What is the difference between SEO and PPC?
SEO builds long-term, free visibility on Google. PPC (ads) gets you seen immediately, but you pay for every click.
7. What is AEO and GEO?
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) help your business get picked up and mentioned by AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, not just traditional search results.
8. How do I choose an SEO agency?
Look for clear pricing, honest timelines, and real explanations of the work being done, not just promises of "page one rankings."
9. How can I rank my business locally?
Through local search marketing, including a well-optimized Google Business Profile, local keywords, and consistent business listings across the web.
